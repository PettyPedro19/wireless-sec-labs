--- RPA Analysis (Trudy's perspective) ---

Trudy's passive capture revealed two consecutive pairs of RPAs, reflecting both
a single RPA rotation event and the dual-earbud architecture of the AirPods Pro 2,
whose left and right earbuds advertise independently with separate BLE radios.
The two earbuds within each pair were distinguished by their UTP byte value
(0x15 vs 0x75) in the Manufacturer Specific Data payload.

All four RPAs were linked to the same physical device using a payload-level
fingerprint filter on the stable fields of the Apple Continuity Protocol
(Type 0x07, Model ID 0x2024), which persist unchanged across RPA rotations.
